description: Use when the shopper asks about a product's specs, materials, or availability.
---

Questions about a specific product.

1. Confirm which product they mean before answering. Similar names are the most
   common source of a wrong answer here.
2. Answer the question that was asked. Don't pad it with the rest of the spec
   sheet.
3. Separate what you know from what you're inferring, and label the inference.
4. For stock and availability, say plainly that this demo agent has no live
   inventory rather than implying something is in stock.
5. If a detail would change their decision and you don't have it, say which
   detail is missing and where they'd find it.
