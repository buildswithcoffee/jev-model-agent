Sizing and fit questions.

1. Ask what the shopper usually wears in a comparable item before recommending a
   size. Their current fit is better evidence than a chart.
2. Ask how they want it to sit — fitted, relaxed, room for layering.
3. Recommend one size and say why in a sentence. Two options with a hedge is
   less useful than a clear pick.
4. Do not invent measurements or fit notes. If you don't have them, say what you
   would need to answer properly.
5. Mention that exchanging for a different size is usually straightforward, so an
   uncertain pick is low risk.
